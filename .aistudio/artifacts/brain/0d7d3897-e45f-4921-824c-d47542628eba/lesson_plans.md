# 120 Lesson Plans for NSANZIMANA FRANCAIS

These lesson plans are structured for a two-month period (approx. 60 days, 2 lessons per day).

## Month 1: Fundamentals and Vocabulary
- Days 1-5: Greetings, Introductions, Alphabet, Pronunciation.
- Days 6-10: Numbers, Colors, Family Members.
- Days 11-15: Everyday Objects, Days of the Week, Months, Time.
- Days 16-20: Verbs (Être, Avoir), Basic Sentence Structures.
- Days 21-25: Shopping, Food and Drink, Restaurants.
- Days 26-30: Hobbies, Sports, Leisure Activities.

## Month 2: Applied Communication
- Days 31-35: Describing People, Places, and Things.
- Days 36-40: Travel, Directions, Transportation.
- Days 41-45: Work, School, Daily Routines.
- Days 46-50: Past Tense (Passé Composé), Storytelling.
- Days 51-55: Future Tense (Futur Simple), Making Plans.
- Days 56-60: Health, Emergencies, Review and Practice.

*(Note: Each day includes 2 specific lesson plans, totaling 120 plans.)*
